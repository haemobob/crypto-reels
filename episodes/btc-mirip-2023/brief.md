# Visual brief — Bitcoin sekarang mirip 2023?

- **Framework**: Remotion (chart-led piece with precise data; nothing here needs HyperFrames).
- **Visual subject**: Bitcoin's own price structure, two windows of real weekly candles. No person or institution is central, so data carries the story (story-visuals rule 9).
- **Visual concept**: *one grid, two eras.* Both periods sit on the same log scale and the same "weeks since the low" axis, so the eye compares shape and timing directly; every phase lands on both charts at the same moment.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`): no 3D world, no order book, no institution. Subject is a BTC structure analogy; environment is a black analytical stage with two side-by-side panels; hero move is a pull-out/push-in from the 2023 chart into today's chart; transitions are camera reframes inside one persistent stage plus one polarity flip.
- **Opening asset hierarchy level**: 3 (real chart data, rebuilt as vector from exchange candles).
- **Visual nouns**: weekly candles (blue up, hollow down); a self-drawing close-price trace; numbered phase markers ①–⑤ with a legend in the headline zone; dashed week guides at 12 and 15; red depth figures; blue level lines; a pulsing "now" dot; dotted SKENARIO paths (blue = reclaim, red = fail).
- **Polarity chapters**: black for the whole analysis (one continuous argument); off-white only for the takeaway thesis (the pivot from "what the chart shows" to "how to use it"); back to black for the closing echo.
- **Hero move**: at "Sekarang" (32.1 s) the camera pulls out of the 2023 zoom through the two-panel view and pushes into today's chart: the one move that connects "what mattered then" to "what matters now". Everything else is gentle push-ins (≤1.07×) and two purposeful zooms.
- **Not used from the brief, and why**: a 3D or glowing Bitcoin coin (optional in the brief; the rulebook lists glowing coins as an anti-pattern and the chart opening was strong without it); particle trails (decorative, rulebook §9); a dotted "projection" without a fail path (shown both, labelled SKENARIO, so it can't read as a forecast).
