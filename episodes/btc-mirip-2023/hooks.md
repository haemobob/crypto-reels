# Hook Lab — Bitcoin sekarang mirip 2023?

## Opening archetype
- **Archetype**: Asset / Market.
- **Real-world anchor**: the actual BTC weekly price charts for both windows (Binance BTCUSDT, 2022-10-31 → 2023-04-17 and 2026-06-08 → 2026-10-19), drawn from exchange data.
- **Designed form it turns into**: two same-scale chart panels on one week grid, annotated phase by phase.
- **Asset hierarchy level used**: 3 (real chart data), rebuilt as vector with identical values (see `sources.md`). No person or institution is central, so no photo or logo.

Scoring 0–5: **C** consequence · **N** novelty · **S** specificity · **E** evidence · **V** one striking visual.

| # | Entry point | Cold open (as spoken) | First-frame headline + anchor | C | N | S | E | V | Total | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence (user's brief) | "Bitcoin sekarang terlihat mirip dengan struktur tahun dua ribu dua puluh tiga." | BITCOIN SEKARANG MIRIP 2023? + two charts | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 19 | Instantly visual; the question mark keeps it from reading as a forecast |
| 2 | Contradiction | "Chart Bitcoin sekarang mirip 2023. Tapi ada satu hal yang beda jauh." | MIRIP, TAPI TIDAK SAMA + charts | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 18 | Strong but leads with the caveat before the viewer has seen the claim |
| 3 | Question | "Minggu ke-16. Di 2023, ini minggu Bitcoin merebut kembali puncaknya. Sekarang?" | MINGGU KE-16 + week grid | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 21 | Most specific, but "minggu ke-16" needs context a stranger lacks in second one |
| 4 | Problem | "Lihat post 'Bitcoin bakal kayak 2023'? Ini yang perlu kamu cek dulu." | CEK INI DULU + charts | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 13 | Generic; weak evidence |
| 5 | Simulation | "Bayangin Bitcoin ngulang 2023 persis. Level ini yang harus ditembus dulu." | $87.400 + level line | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 18 | Concrete, but "ngulang persis" leans toward hopium |

**Selected hook:** #1, the user's opening, with the on-screen question mark.
**Why it wins for this audience:** a stranger gets the claim from the picture alone (two charts, same shape) and the question mark plus "Bukan berarti hasilnya pasti sama" in the next breath keeps it honest.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #2 (contradiction).
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #3 (week 16).
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: breakout week 7, peak week 12 and pullback low week 15 line up exactly, while the pullback depth does not.

## A/B hook variants (same body, 0–8 s)
- **Variant A, consequence:** "Kalau Bitcoin benar-benar ngulang 2023, ada satu level yang harus direbut dulu."
- **Variant B, contradiction/scale (selected):** "Bitcoin sekarang terlihat mirip dengan struktur tahun dua ribu dua puluh tiga. Bukan berarti hasilnya pasti sama."
- Silent comparison: B's first frame reads with sound off from the headline and the two charts; A needs the narration to explain "satu level".
- Variant used for release: B, matching the user's brief. A stays available for a Trial Reels test (user's call).

## First-frame test
Headline is on screen and legible at frame 0 (rises in over the first frames); narration starts within the first frames; no logo before the hook; real chart data on screen by ~1.5 s; no abstract 3D at all.
