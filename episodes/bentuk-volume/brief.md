# Brief — bentuk-volume

- **Visual subject:** Bitcoin's real last five days (Binance 5-minute candles), each day with its own volume profile, plus OKX open interest for the bounce day. Data carries the story; no person or institution is involved.
- **Visual concept:** "a week of shapes". One continuous chart of five days. Each day's profile grows from its right edge with a gold POC bar and a big letter (b, P, D) or a "PANJANG & TIPIS" tag. The frame travels to the day being described and finally pulls back to show all four shapes side by side.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`):
  - poc-bitcoin used one daily chart with two range profiles and one reframe.
  - This one changes the timescale (intraday 5-minute data), the profile (one per day, on the day's right edge), the camera (several short data-space moves, each landing on the day being named) and the evidence layer (an open-interest panel under the chart).
  - Gold stays as the accent for volume levels, for continuity with the previous point-of-control Reel.
- **Framework:** Remotion (2D SVG).
- **Polarity:** navy throughout; one argument on one chart.
- **Camera:** data-space reframes only. The chart re-scales, so type and strokes keep their size.
  - The sequence is 15 Nov → 12 Nov → 12–13 Nov → 14 Nov → 15–16 Nov → 16 Nov plus "today" → the whole week.
  - Each move is about 1.2 s and lands on the day the narration names. These are more moves than the restrained default; the installed motion-doctrine skill allows a continuous camera, and every move here answers "where is the audience going?".
- **Hero move:** the final pull-back to the whole week at "Bentuknya bukan sinyal", where the four shapes are seen together.
- **Asset hierarchy:** level 3, real charts and data. No photo or logo applies.
