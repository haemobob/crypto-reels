# Retention brief — bentuk-volume

## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian Bitcoin holders and traders who watched this week's drop and the bounce from $62K.
- **Their question:** "Is the bounce real? Are buyers back?"
- **What exactly they learn:**
  - how to read the four shapes of a daily volume profile (b, long and thin, P, D) as a sign of who was in control;
  - that a P-shaped bounce with falling open interest is more likely short covering than new buying.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** it gives a check they can do on any daily chart before they chase a bounce, plus today's two edges ($63.9K and $65.7K).
- **What they keep:** "fat = agreed price, thin = price just passed through", and the shape tells you who's in control and where the limit is.

## V3 brief
- **Consequence:** chasing a bounce that is mostly short covering can mean buying where the support is weakest.
- **Counterintuitive fact:** BTC rose 5.4% on 15 Nov while OKX open interest fell 3.2%. Price went up while positions shrank.
- **Evidence:** the real 5-minute BTC chart and the profile for each day; OKX hourly open interest.
- **One-sentence payoff:** the shape is not a signal, but it tells you who's in control and where the limit is.
- **Misconception:** that a shape is a buy or sell signal, or that every bounce means "buyers are back".
- **Share trigger:** the friend who wants to buy the bounce.
- **Emotional shape:** doubt → surprise (open interest fell) → clarity (the four shapes) → a calm practical check.
- **Chosen length:** about 55–62 s (window 45–70 s). Four shapes on four real days, plus the open-interest check, can't fit in 35 s without dropping the evidence.
