# Brief — burry-nvidia (visual plan)

- **Framework:** Remotion, 2D (SVG in screen space from a stage camera). No 3D: the story is carried by real photos and real data, and the 3D pipeline costs ~75 min per render.
- **Visual subject:** the real Nvidia price line (it *is* the trade), plus Michael Burry's real photos for the who. No famous face for attention: Burry is the story's actor. No Huang photo (no licensed photo obtained; the data carries the drama).
- **Visual concept:** *the trade on one line.* One continuous camera travels Nvidia's real daily line from the 13F disclosure to Huang's return. The entry price ($206.88) is a blue rail across the year: red fills the gap when price is above it (a short losing), green when below (winning). The rail is the spine: drawn at the bet, it measures the loss, the profit and finally the leverage lesson.
- **Chapters / polarity:** black market board (the crash) → the premiere photo → off-white archive (2005–2008, the past) → black line world (the trade). Polarity flips mark the move into the past and back.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`): first person-led story since satu-meja, but photos are used full-bleed in the band (not framed cards on a map); first stock-price story; one camera on one real line instead of a chart room with cuts; the off-white chapter holds a real macro index (Case-Shiller), not a crypto chart.
- **Hero move:** none in 3D; the long track along the line from $207 up to $243 and down to $125, then the pull-back to the whole year for the lesson.
- **Real assets (hierarchy):** real photos (premiere, desk) → real data (Yahoo, FRED) → designed cards (13F summary card, SIMULASI card).
