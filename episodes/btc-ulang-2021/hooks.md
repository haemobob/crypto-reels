# Hook Lab — btc-ulang-2021

## Opening archetype (opening rules)
- **Archetype:** Asset / Market (a chart claim; no person).
- **Real-world anchor:** today's real Bitcoin daily chart (Binance BTC/USDT, Jul 2025 – 9 Nov 2026), on screen from frame 0 with the source line.
- **Designed form it turns into:** the chart becomes the base of a light table; the 2021–23 chart arrives on a tracing sheet.
- **Asset hierarchy level used:** 3 (real market data).

Scoring 0–5: **C** consequence · **N** novelty · **S** specificity · **E** evidence · **V** visual.

| # | Entry point | Cold open (as spoken) | First-frame headline + anchor | C | N | S | E | V | Total | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question | "Katanya, Bitcoin lagi ngulang siklus dua ribu dua puluh satu, langkah demi langkah. Dan ini dip terakhir sebelum all time high baru." | BITCOIN NGULANG 2021? · today's chart + KLAIM VIRAL chip | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 20 | The claim people saw, stated fairly, then tested |
| 2 | Contradiction | "Urutannya sama persis. Ukurannya? Jauh lebih kecil." | URUTAN SAMA, UKURAN BEDA | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 21 | Strongest twist, but needs the claim first |
| 3 | Evidence | "Tahun 2023, Bitcoin balik 42% dari rally-nya, lalu nggak pernah semurah itu lagi. Sekarang, 42% itu ada di tujuh puluh lima ribu dolar." | 42% = ±$75 RB | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 22 | Concrete and live; works better as the mid-film reward |
| 4 | Problem | "Lihat post 'ini dip terakhir sebelum ATH'? Cek dulu tiga hal." | DIP TERAKHIR? | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 13 | Generic |
| 5 | Simulation | "Tumpuk chart 2021 di atas chart sekarang…" | 2021 DI ATAS 2026 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 17 | Visual, but abstract as an opener |

**Selected hook:** #1 (question: the claim itself, fairly stated), with #2's twist as the second reward (~17 s) and #3 as the mid-film reward (~32 s).
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #4 → #1.
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #3.
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: same order, much smaller and faster moves; the "same" level is being tested now and was already undercut intraday.
