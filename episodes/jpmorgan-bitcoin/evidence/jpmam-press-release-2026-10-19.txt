Morgan Releases 2027 Long Term Capital Market Assumptions Projecting 7.0% Return for 60/40 Portfolio Over 10-15 Years Published: 10/19/2026 Greater artificial intelligence (AI)-driven productivity, higher corporate profits and bond yields drives long-term growth outlook A balanced portfolio with a 30% alternatives allocation can increase returns up to 7.5%, and boost Sharpe ratios by 21% when compared with a simple 60/40 approach. Higher real yields drive global aggregate bond forecasts to 5.3%, the highest in two decades. Resilient profits increase U.S. large cap equity forecasts by 80bps, leading to developed markets return forecasts overtaking emerging markets. NEW YORK, NY, October 19, 2026 — J.P. Morgan Asset Management today released its 2027 Long-Term Capital Market Assumptions (LTCMAs), providing a comprehensive 10-15-year outlook for returns and risks across asset classes as inflation uncertainty and market volatility remain elevated.

In this 31st edition of the LTCMAs, the forecast annual return for a USD 60/40 stock-bond portfolio over the next 10–15 years rises to 7.0%, but higher stock-bond correlations demand further diversification. While labor and demographic constraints weigh on long‑term growth, the report sees broader AI investment and adoption across industries providing a near-term boost to profits and increasing productivity over the long-term. The report highlights opportunities to diversify by spreading equity exposure across regions and alternatives, particularly hedge funds and real assets For investors embracing a 60/40+ portfolio, with 30% in diversified alternatives, the projected return increases to 7.5% and the Sharpe ratio gets a 21% lift over the simple 60/40 approach.

“Our Long-Term Capital Market Assumptions help investors navigate markets reshaped by economic nationalism, accelerating technology, and the rewiring of capital flows” said John Bilton, Global Head of Strategic Research for Multi-Asset Solutions, J.P. Morgan Asset Management. “This year’s outlook sees stronger long-term growth and renewed opportunities to build resilient portfolios through diversification, combining higher global bond yields with alternatives and real assets for differentiated returns and inflation resilience.”

“The global economy is entering a powerful investment cycle, with AI adoption and rising productivity supporting a stronger growth outlook,” said David Kelly, Chief Global Strategist, J.P. Morgan Asset Management. “At the same time, demographic headwinds, large government deficits, and a more complex geopolitical backdrop suggest inflation will remain more volatile over our forecast horizon.”

"Every year the LTCMAs give us the foundation for building client portfolios, and this year that has never mattered more. Return matters, but it is no longer the whole story,” said Grace Peters, Global Co-Head of Investment Strategy for J.P. Morgan Private Bank. “In today's environment, optimizing risk and return together is the imperative, and with dispersion wider than it has been in years, investors anchored to a disciplined strategic asset allocation will find this a more compelling environment than the headlines suggest."
