# Beat sheet — JPMorgan: Bitcoin 10% per tahun, tapi 55%

Topic: J.P. Morgan Asset Management's 2027 Long-Term Capital Market Assumptions (19 Oct 2026) give Bitcoin a return forecast for the first time: 10% a year over 10–15 years, above U.S. large-cap stocks (7.5%) and gold (5.5%).
Viewer payoff: the headline number has a second number behind it, 55% volatility.
