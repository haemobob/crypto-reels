# Hook Lab — jpmorgan-bitcoin

Scores are an internal editorial rubric, not a predictor of virality.

## Opening archetype
- **Archetype:** News/Document with an Authority contradiction.
- **Real-world anchor:** the official 2027 Long-Term Capital Market Assumptions cover from J.P. Morgan Asset Management's LTCMA page (with its wordmark, unaltered), on screen from about 2.4 s; Dimon's 2017 quote credited to CNBC.
- **Designed form it turns into:** a 3D podium ranking Bitcoin, U.S. stocks and gold by projected return.
- **Asset hierarchy level:** 3 (official document) with 2 (the firm's wordmark on it).

Scoring 0–5: **C** consequence · **N** novelty · **S** specificity · **E** evidence · **V** one striking visual.

**Selected hook:** #1, then #4 as the bridge at about 10 s and #3 as the reward at about 30 s.
**Why it wins:** it states the news, the authority and a real contradiction within 4 seconds, with a real document on screen.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #3 (rupiah outcomes).
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** "Rata-rata dua puluh koma sembilan persen, tapi yang tersisa cuma sepuluh. Itu yang namanya volatility drag."
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: the "fraud" bank projects Bitcoin first, yet keeps it out of its own model portfolios.

## A/B hook variants (same body, 0–8 s)
- **Variant A — consequence:** #3, "Sepuluh juta di Bitcoin, sepuluh tahun…" over the scenario fan.
- **Variant B — authority/contradiction:** #1 (release version).
- Variant used: B; A is kept for a Trial Reels test if the account supports it.

## First-frame test
Frame 0: the Bitcoin coin with a red "FRAUD — 2017" stamp under the headline DULU "PENIPUAN". SEKARANG JUARA?, readable in about a second; narration starts in the first frames; the real report cover is on screen by 2.4 s; no abstract-only 3D before the anchor.
