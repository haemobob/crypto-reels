# Retention brief — jpmorgan-bitcoin

Written before any full script (viewer-value directive §1 and retention rules, Rule 1).

## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian investors and crypto holders who are choosing between Bitcoin, stocks and gold, and who will see the headline "JPMorgan: Bitcoin kalahkan saham dan emas" everywhere this week.
- **Viewer's problem or misconception:** reading "10% a year, more than stocks and gold" as a smooth, likely 10% a year.
- **What exactly they learn:** (1) what JPMorgan actually projected and how it compares (10% vs 7.5% vs 5.5%, a 10–15-year projection, not a promise); (2) the second number, 55% volatility, and why big swings eat returns (−50% then +50% = −25%; a 20.9% simple average leaves 10% compounded); (3) the range: in JPMorgan's scenarios Rp10 juta over 10 years ends anywhere from about Rp3,6 juta to Rp48 juta; (4) this already happened: $126K → $58K between Oct 2025 and Jun 2026; (5) JPMorgan itself doesn't put Bitcoin in its model portfolios.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** gold and stocks are the benchmarks Indonesians actually compare with; the headline invites buying on the 10% while ignoring the drawdowns that make most people sell at the bottom.
- **What they keep:** a mental model ("return = hadiah, volatilitas = harga tiketnya") and a one-question self-check ("kalau turun setengah, lo kuat nahan?").

## Discovery sequence
- **Early useful insight (≤ 8–12 s):** the actual numbers: Bitcoin 10%, U.S. stocks 7.5%, gold 5.5% per year over 10–15 years, from the report itself.
- **Visual demonstration:** the podium (ranking by return) turns out to be only the finish line; behind it, the tracks: gold and stocks smooth, Bitcoin a rollercoaster. A stack of 100 coins halves and regrows to 75. Scenario ribbons fan out from Rp10 juta. Real weekly chart of the 2025–26 fall.
- **Final takeaway:** return is the prize, volatility is the ticket price; ask whether you could hold through a halving.
- **Real assets:** the report cover (J.P. Morgan's public LTCMA page), JPMorgan's own tables (rebuilt with identical values), real Binance weekly prices. Nothing needed from the user.

## Retention brief (V3)
- **Audience:** Indonesian retail investors and crypto holders, 20–40, who compare Bitcoin with gold (emas) and stocks.
- **Consequence:** the 10% headline can push people into Bitcoin expecting a smooth ride; the same forecast says to expect swings that cut your money in half.
- **Counterintuitive fact:** the bank whose CEO called Bitcoin "a fraud" now projects it to beat stocks and gold, yet still won't put it in its own model portfolios.
- **Evidence:** J.P. Morgan Asset Management, 2027 LTCMA, 19 Oct 2026: BTC 10% compound, 55% volatility, 20.9% arithmetic mean, scenarios −9.7%/17%; U.S. large cap 7.5%, gold 5.5%; Binance: $126.2K → $57.8K (−54%).
- **One-sentence payoff:** a higher expected return comes with a far wider range of outcomes, so the useful question is not "which wins?" but "could I hold through the drops?".
- **Misconception:** that "10% per year" means roughly 10% every year.
- **Share trigger:** the friend who forwards "JPMorgan says Bitcoin beats gold" as a reason to go all in, or the one who holds only gold.
- **Emotional shape:** surprise (contradiction) → curiosity (the second number) → clarity (mechanism and range) → a calm, honest check. No fear manufactured.
- **Chosen length:** about 55–60 s (window 45–62 s). The mechanism (drag), the range and the real proof each need their beat; nothing else is added.
