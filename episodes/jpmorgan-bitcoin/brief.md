# Visual brief — JPMorgan: Bitcoin 10% per tahun, tapi 55%

- **Framework:** Remotion + React Three Fiber (`@remotion/three`) with the shared 3D kit from bitcoin-shutdown (`src/three/`); 2D for the real price chart and type. The user asked for 3D where it helps, more visualization and varied camera angles (blackrock v2 feedback).
- **Visual subject:** data, not a person. The story is a forecast and the risk behind it, so the subjects are the three assets (a Bitcoin coin, a stock tile, a gold bar), JPMorgan's real report, and Bitcoin's real price path. Dimon appears only as a credited quote, not a face (no famous faces for attention).
- **Visual concept:** *the podium and the ride.* The forecast is a podium (Bitcoin on the top step at 10%). The hero move swings behind it: the podium is only the finish line, and behind it run the tracks each asset rides to get there: gold and stocks gentle ramps, Bitcoin a rollercoaster. The prize (return) and the ticket price (volatility) are one image, and the ending returns to it.
- **Real assets first:** credited 2017 quote (frame 0) → the real 2027 LTCMA cover (3.7 s, level 3 document with the firm's wordmark, unaltered) → JPMorgan's numbers rebuilt with identical values → real Binance weekly chart (46.6–54.4 s).
- **Beats and worlds:**
  1. *Stamp* (0–3.7 s): close-up of the 3D coin, red FRAUD stamp, title DULU "PENIPUAN". SEKARANG JUARA?
  2. *Report* (3.7–11.4 s): the real cover as a physical report on a dark desk, whole, then a push to its title.
  3. *Podium* (11.4–17.4 s): podium rises; numbers count in on each word.
  4. *Ride* (17.4–37.5 s, hero move): crane up and around behind the podium; tracks; a coin cart on Bitcoin's track; a stack of 100 coins halves to 50 and regrows to 75 (−25%); a ghost extension on Bitcoin's step shows 20.9% and evaporates to the 10% step.
  5. *Fan* (37.5–46.6 s): from one point (Rp10 juta) three ribbons fan out over ten year markers: Rp48,1 jt / Rp25,9 jt / Rp3,6 jt, SIMULASI.
  6. *Real chart* (46.6–54.4 s, 2D): Binance weekly candles Jan 2025–Oct 2026; peak $126,2K (Okt 2025), low $57,8K (Jun 2026), −54%.
  7. *Ring* (54.4–58.3 s): JPMorgan's headline 60/40 portfolio as a 3D ring (SAHAM 60%, OBLIGASI 40%) with the Bitcoin coin waiting outside it, BELUM MASUK; ILUSTRASI; "bitcoin is not included as an allocation".
  8. *Payoff* (58.3–68.4 s): back to the podium-and-ride wide shot: RETURN = HADIAHNYA / VOLATILITAS = HARGA TIKETNYA, then KALAU TURUN SETENGAH, LO KUAT NAHAN?
- **Polarity:** black/navy throughout (one continuous "night arena"); one off-white chart chapter for the real-data proof (46.6–54.4 s) marks the switch from model to reality.
- **Hero move:** the crane up over the podium as the tracks draw in behind it (17.4–21.9 s), then a hard cut to the reverse angle from the far end of the tracks: the podium is only the finish line (21.9 s). The payoff returns to that reverse angle. Supporting: documentary push on the report, fast flight to the coin stack, dolly-out on the fan.
