- **Visual subject:** the real BTC/USDT daily chart and two volume profiles computed from real hourly volume. Data carries the story; no person or institution is involved.
- **Visual concept:** "two gold lines". Each profile grows sideways from its range, and its busiest bar turns gold and stretches across the chart as a dashed line. Every later move is then read against those two lines.

- **Framework:** Remotion (2D SVG).
- **Polarity:** navy throughout. There is no chapter flip because it is one argument on one chart.
- **Hero move:** the reframe at "Agustus…" (27.8 s), where the rally between the two lines is about to be described.
- **Asset hierarchy:** level 3, a real chart from real data. No photo or logo applies.
