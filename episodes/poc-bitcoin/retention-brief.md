# Retention brief — poc-bitcoin

## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian Bitcoin holders and new traders watching the drop from $87K to $62K.
- **Their question:** "Why did it stop there, and what's the level now?" The common belief is that these turns are random, or "just support".
- **What exactly they learn:** what a point of control is (the price where the most volume traded in a range), how to read one off a volume profile, and that Bitcoin's two latest turns happened at two of them.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** it gives two concrete BTC levels to watch now ($64K below, $88K above), plus the next one if $64K breaks ($57.8K), instead of guessing.
- **What they keep:** a mental model ("where most volume traded, price often reacts") and three numbers.

## V3 brief
- **Audience:** BTC holders and traders on Instagram Indonesia, mostly retail.
- **Consequence:** the two levels that framed the last two moves are the ones to watch now.
- **Counterintuitive fact:** the top and the bottom were not random. Both landed within about 3% of a volume level that already existed months earlier (+0.5% under the upper POC, −3.3% under the lower one, with the close back above it).
- **Evidence:** Binance hourly volume profiles; $87,396 on 21 Sep against a POC of ≈ $87,900; $62,006 on 15 Nov against ≈ $64,100.
- **One-sentence payoff:** watch $64K below and $88K above; if $64K breaks, the July low of $57.8K is next.
- **Misconception:** that a POC is a guarantee or a prediction. It is a place where price often reacts. Shown as "BACAAN KAMI, BUKAN RAMALAN".
- **Share trigger:** the friend asking "BTC lanjut turun nggak?"; this gives them levels instead of a guess.
- **Emotional shape:** curiosity (coincidence?) → clarity (the profile) → satisfaction (it lines up twice) → calm (three numbers).
- **Chosen length:** about 60 s (window 50–72 s). Two profiles plus two moves need about 45 s; the payoff and the next level take the rest. Nothing padded.
