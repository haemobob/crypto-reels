# Hook Lab — lantai-hammer (rebuild of tiga-hammer, 22 Nov 2026)

## Why the rebuild
The tiga-hammer story taught the B pattern for 32 s and then undercut it ("bukan rumus"). Its jargon was never pictured, and its actors didn't act. The data supports a different, more useful story: a hammer gives you a **floor** (the wick tip). The floor broke within 24 h in 13 of 28 BTC hammers, it sits a median 0.77% under the close, and price rose that same distance before breaking it 17 times vs 9.

## Storylines
1. **Two actors, two choices (picked).** Raka and Dina buy the same real hammer (14 Nov 2026, 22:00 WIB). Dina's stop sits under the wick tip; Raka has none. Then the crowd of 28, the floor idea, the numbers, and the rule. Device: one line (the floor) as the spine, from hook to payoff.
2. **Myth turned over.** "Hammer = dasar?" → 13/28 broke within a day → so use it as a floor. Strong, but it opens on a debate, not a picture.
3. **A rule then a worked example.** The rule first, then 14 Nov. Clear, but it gives the answer before the stakes.

## Opening archetype
**Asset/Market.** The real anchor is the real 14 Nov hammer on BTCUSDT H1 (Binance), shown whole, with the two actors standing in front of it.
