# Retention brief — lantai-hammer

## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian crypto traders who buy candlestick patterns, above all newer ones who "buy the hammer".
- **Problem / misconception:** "A hammer means the bottom is in." They buy it without knowing where they're wrong.
- **What exactly they learn:**
  - the hammer's wick tip is a floor: the price level where the idea is wrong;
  - it is usually close (median 0.77% on BTC H1);
  - it broke within a day in nearly half of 28 real hammers;
  - so a stop goes under it, sized from that distance.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** BTC trades 24 hours, and stops get skipped overnight. 14 Nov showed a −5.7% night against a −1% planned loss.
- **What they keep:** one rule: "beli tanpa lantai itu berharap, pakai lantai itu rencana" (where is my floor, and how far away is it).

## Discovery sequence
- **Early insight (≤ 8 s):** the difference between the two traders is one line.
- **Demonstration:** the real 14 Nov path (break, bounce, overnight fall), the 28 floors as a crowd, the floor distance against the move up.
- **Final takeaway:** the hammer is not a buy signal; it tells you where you're wrong.
- **Real assets:** Binance BTCUSDT data only (no user assets needed).
