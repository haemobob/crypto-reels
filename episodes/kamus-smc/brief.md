# Visual brief — Kamus SMC: 18 istilah di 1 chart Bitcoin

- **Framework:** Remotion + React Three Fiber (`@remotion/three`) with the shared 3D kit (`src/three/`). The reference Reel (@cuanterusidn, De_V4s2StNA) was used for analysis only (`reference-analysis.md`).
- **Visual subject:** data, no person: one real chart, Binance BTC/USDT daily candles from 20 May to 28 Oct 2026 (162 candles), rebuilt as 3D candles with identical values. The terms are a way of reading a chart, so the chart is the subject.
- **Visual concept:** *one chart, read five times.* The same real chart gains one layer per stage: swing tags and the trend line (1), break levels (2), piles of stop orders beyond the highs and lows, the sweep wick that spears the pile, and the trap (3), the order-block slab and the glowing gap (4), the range cut at 50% into premium and discount volumes (5). The payoff pulls back to the fully read chart. The opening's pile of 18 labels (the confusion) returns at the end in order (the map).
- **Why 3D:** depth carries information here: liquidity is a *pile* of orders behind a level (volume), the order block and the gap are *zones* that extend forward in time, premium/discount are two volumes; the camera can look along the time axis for the trend and from the side for the piles. Candles stay frontal and readable whenever a term is defined.
- **Real assets first:** the real chart from frame 0 with its source line; every tag carries its real date and price.
- **Beats** (Orus timings):
  1. *Cold open* (0–9 s): whole chart; ORDER BLOCK / FVG / SWEEP land on their spots, then all 18 labels pile on ("pusing"), then clear; the five-stage index appears.
  2. *Struktur* (9–17.7 s): along the May–June slide (LH 26 May, LL 5 Jun, LH 15 Jun, LL 25 Jun), then the July–August climb (HH 21 Jul, HL 1 Aug, HH 28 Aug).
  3. *Perubahan* (17.7–26.2 s): BOS down on 24 Jun (close under the 18 Jun low); CHoCH up on 14 Jul (close over the 6 Jul high).
  4. *Liquidity* (26.2–45.5 s): stop piles grow under lows and over highs; SSL under 58.115; BSL over the equal highs 65.623 / 65.600; the 1 Jul wick spears the SSL pile (57.800) and closes 60.024: SWEEP; inducement at the 9 Aug minor high.
  5. *Area entry* (45.5–59.8 s): order block 16 Aug; the gap 65.059–68.902 from the 19 Aug surge; an illustrated return into it (mitigation, ILUSTRASI); pull back to show the gap still open through 28 Oct.
  6. *Mahal atau murah* (59.8–71.6 s): range 57.800 → 66.956 halved at 62.378; premium above, discount below; the 1 Aug low 62.275 lands in discount.
  7. *Payoff* (71.6–79.5 s): the whole read chart; STRUKTUR → LIQUIDITY → SWEEP → ENTRY with each layer pulsing in turn; CARA BACA, BUKAN JAMINAN · STOP LOSS TETAP WAJIB.
- **Polarity:** one dark navy chart room throughout (the chart must stay readable); no off-white chapter. Turns are marked by the stage index and camera angle changes.
- **Hero move:** the dive along the time axis into the 1 Jul candle as its wick spears the stop pile (37–40 s). Supporting: low angle along the slide (stage 1), side angle on the piles (stage 3), three-quarter on the slab and gap (stage 4), pull-back to the whole chart (payoff).
- **Labels at the claim:** source line BINANCE BTC/USDT · HARIAN throughout with CONTOH PEMBACAAN · BUKAN SINYAL; ZONA STOP = PERKIRAAN during liquidity; ILUSTRASI on the mitigation path.
- **Colours:** up candles brand electric blue, down candles slate; stop piles red (risk), the sweep flash white; premium red-tinted, discount green-tinted (meaning only); everything else blue/white.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`): no podium, globe, devices or coins; first 3D chart world; first episode built on one dataset end to end; new hero move (dive along the time axis into a wick); new transition grammar (layers accumulating on one object instead of cutting between worlds).
- **Missing assets:** none needed from the user.
