## Why it holds attention
- A clear promise with a number (18 terms, 5 stages) and a visible progress bar: the viewer always knows how far along they are.
- One term card at a time, each with a short Indonesian gloss; each stage closes with a checklist.
- A save-worthy summary grid at the end (a glossary people keep).

## What it misses (and our version fixes)
- Every chart is an abstract zigzag: no real market, no dates, no prices. Nothing shows that these patterns exist on a real chart, or how messy they look there.
- States SMC readings as facts ("liquidity itu tumpukan stop loss", "FVG sering diisi balik") with no caveat; SMC terms are not standardised, readings are subjective, and the framework's edge has no rigorous public testing (`research.md`).
- Ends on a save/comment request.

## Our adaptation
Same promise (18 terms, 5 stages, in the order a trader uses them) on **one real Bitcoin chart** (Binance BTC/USDT daily, May–Sep 2026) read five times: each stage adds its layer to the same chart, and the last frame is the fully read chart. Cryptonite design (navy/black, brand blue, Inter Tight / JetBrains Mono, micro captions), a 3D chart world with real candles, the evidence behind "liquidity" (Osler 2003), caveats at the claim, and a payoff checklist instead of a CTA.
