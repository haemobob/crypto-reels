# Hook Lab — kamus-smc

At least five meaningfully different Indonesian cold opens, using at least three different entry points. Scores are an internal editorial rubric, not a predictor of virality.

## Opening archetype (opening rules)
- **Archetype:** Asset / Market (the topic is a way of reading a chart; the subject is the chart itself).
- **Real-world anchor:** the real Binance BTC/USDT daily chart, May–Sep 2026 (S9), on screen from the first second, with its source line.
- **Designed form it turns into:** a 3D chart world: the same candles as physical columns on a dark floor, annotated stage by stage.
- **Asset hierarchy level used:** 3 (real chart data, rebuilt with identical values).

Scoring 0–5: **C** personal consequence · **N** novelty/curiosity · **S** specificity · **E** evidence/trust · **V** one striking visual.

**Selected hook:** #1 (problem), with #2 as the first real event inside stage 3.
**Why it wins for this audience:** it names the feeling (jargon overload) and promises the cure in one sentence, with a real chart on screen from frame 0; it keeps the "18 terms, 5 stages" value the reference proved, and adds the real-chart difference.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** "Pernah denger trader bilang 'harga lagi nyari liquidity'? Ini artinya, di chart Bitcoin asli."
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #2, the 1 July sweep: "57.800, lalu tutup 60.024. Sweep, CHoCH, BOS: satu chart, delapan belas istilah."
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: the August gap that a "gaps get filled" reading expects is still open; equal highs are a target, not a wall.
