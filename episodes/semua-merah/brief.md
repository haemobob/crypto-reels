# Visual brief — Semua merah. Uangnya ke mana?

- **Framework:** Remotion + React Three Fiber with the shared 3D kit. Reference (@mak, DfILOszputn) used for analysis only.
- **Visual subject:** data, no person: the real 30 Oct 2026 closing moves as a market board.
- **Visual concept:** *the board behind the red.* The film opens on four red tiles (S&P 500, emas, perak, Bitcoin). A trade is shown in front of the board as a coin and a cash stack crossing between a seller and a buyer (the money only changes hands; the price tag is what drops). Then the camera pulls back and the board turns out to be bigger: green tiles (US bonds, oil, energy, staples) and a dollar that slipped. Finally each red tile gets its own trigger card. The payoff returns to the whole board with the two questions.
- **Real assets first:** every tile's number is the real close (S1); the S&P 500 and Bitcoin tiles carry their real daily charts; the source line credits CappNotes / Binance / FRED.
- **Beats** (Orus timings): hook 0–6 s (four red tiles) · question 6–7.7 · myth 7.7–10.6 (a CASH box) · trade 10.6–19.9 (seller ↔ buyer, ILUSTRASI) · green 19.9–33 (pull-back reveal: bonds, oil, sectors, dollar) · why 33–52.5 (Intel card, Fed-hike card, Binance + liquidation cards) · payoff 52.5–60.5 (two questions, green tiles then the liquidation card pulse).
- **Polarity:** dark navy trading room throughout; colour carries meaning only (red down, green up, grey for the flat dollar).
- **Hero move:** the pull-back from the four red tiles to the full board that reveals the green tiles (19.9–22 s).
- **Labels at the claim:** source line per section; ILUSTRASI on the trade; "KATA ANALIS · PELUANG DARI PASAR PREDIKSI" on the Fed card; "DILAPORKAN · ±$3 M DIBEKUKAN" on the Binance card.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`): no chart world, podium or globe; first market-board composition; first trade shown as two objects crossing; hero move is a pull-back reveal of a hidden grid.
- **Missing assets:** none.
