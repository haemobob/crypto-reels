## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian crypto and gold holders who saw everything go red on 30 October and the viral "the money fled to cash" explanation.
- **Viewer's problem or misconception:** that falling prices mean money "left" to somewhere (cash), and that everything fell for one reason.
- **What exactly they learn:** (1) a falling price is a trade at a lower price: every seller had a buyer, so the money changed hands; (2) on 30 Oct not everything was red: US bonds, oil, energy and staples rose and the dollar slipped: that's where the "safety" money shows up; (3) the red tiles had different triggers: Intel's −28.6% for chips, Fed-hike expectations for gold and silver, Binance's ≈ $6.3B security incident and >$1.5B of forced liquidations for crypto.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** red days are when people panic-sell or buy a story; knowing how to read them (what's green, who's forced to sell) is a reusable skill.
- **What they keep:** a two-question check for the next red day: *apa yang hijau? siapa yang terpaksa jual?*
