## What it gets wrong (checked against the 30 Oct 2026 close, `research.md`)
- **Bonds didn't dump:** Treasuries rallied; the 10-year yield fell 4 bps to 4.94%.
- **The dollar didn't rise:** the dollar index slipped 0.12% to 101.92.
- **"The money is hiding in cash":** when a share or a coin is sold, a buyer pays for it: the cash changes hands; falling prices don't move money "into cash" in aggregate. Some money did move: into bonds and defensive sectors.
- **One cause for everything:** the drops had different triggers that day (Intel's guidance for chips, Fed-hike expectations for metals, the Binance incident and forced liquidations for crypto).
- Ends on "you'll make serious money" and a follow/Discord CTA.
