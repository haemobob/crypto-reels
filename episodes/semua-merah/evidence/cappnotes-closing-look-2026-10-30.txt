Frank Cappelleri Oct 30, 2026 4 1 Share Summary 🔴 Intel ( INTC ) suffered a historic collapse after weak guidance, setting off a global chip rout and handing the S&P 500 its worst day since June. AI spending worries hit Amazon ( AMZN ), as investors punished heavy capex without matching cash flow. Defensive sectors held up , with energy, staples and utilities rising while technology took the brunt of the selling. Bonds and oil both rallied , as a tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz lifted crude and safety demand pulled yields lower. Gold and silver slid to their lowest levels in about a year , and a massive Binance hack rattled crypto.
