Frank Cappelleri Oct 29, 2026 2 Share Summary 🔴 Alphabet ( GOOGL ) suffered its worst day ever after a rare miss, dragging the Nasdaq and snapping the S&P 500’s three-day winning streak. Most stocks actually rose , as health care, real estate and staples led a broad rotation away from tech and communication services. Hotter inflation and strong growth came in close to forecasts, and Treasury yields fell anyway as investors sought safety. After the bell, Amazon ( AMZN ) and Intel ( INTC ) slid on misses and soft guidance , keeping AI spending worries front and center. Christine Lagarde said she will leave the ECB early as the bank held rates and kept a December hike in play.
