# Hook Lab — semua-merah

## Opening archetype (opening rules)
- **Archetype:** Asset / Market (a market event; no person).
- **Real-world anchor:** the real 30 Oct 2026 closing moves (S&P 500, gold, silver, Bitcoin) as a market board, with the real Bitcoin and S&P daily charts in their tiles; source line CappNotes / Binance.
- **Designed form it turns into:** the board becomes the stage for the trade illustration, then widens to the full board (red and green).
- **Asset hierarchy level used:** 3 (real market data).

Scoring 0–5: **C** consequence · **N** novelty · **S** specificity · **E** evidence · **V** visual.

**Selected hook:** #1 (question), with #2's twist as the second reward (12–15 s) and #5's trade as the first answer.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #3 → #1.
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #2.
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: bonds rallied and the dollar fell on the "everything dumps" day; falling prices don't move money anywhere.
