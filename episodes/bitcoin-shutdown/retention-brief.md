# Retention brief — Bitcoin akan segera shutdown?

The user's production brief (24 Oct 2026) fixes the topic, the narration and the 65–80 s target. This brief records the viewer value and the retention plan behind it.

## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian crypto holders, investors and traders aged 18–40, many holding BTC on an exchange app, some in self-custody.
- **Viewer's problem or misconception:** "If the internet goes down, my Bitcoin is gone", or the opposite, "Bitcoin is decentralised, so outages don't affect me at all".
- **What exactly they learn:** (1) a Bitcoin transaction is a signed message that must *reach* other nodes and get into a block, so a lost connection *delays* it; (2) a local outage doesn't stop the global network, but a split network can't pass transactions between the pieces; (3) the coins stay recorded on the blockchain, and the private key is what keeps them yours; (4) Blockstream's satellite can deliver blockchain data with no internet, but only one way; (5) coins on an exchange are only as reachable as the exchange's systems.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian investor:** this week's real events (the UK's three-day preparedness advice, the global PHP flaw that took sites offline) show that outages are an everyday risk, and most Indonesian holders use exchange apps.
- **What they keep:** a mental model with two layers. *Owning* Bitcoin (on-chain, guarded by your key) is not the same as *using* it (it needs a working path to the network, and for exchange accounts, the exchange itself).

## Discovery sequence
- **Early useful insight (by 8–12 s):** a real government is telling households to prepare for three days without power, water, phones or the internet (GOV.UK, 22 Oct 2026).
- **Visual demonstration:** a transaction travelling node to node over a real map of reachable Bitcoin nodes (Bitnodes snapshot); then a local outage and a partition, shown on the same map, with the transaction stuck.
- **Final takeaway:** "Internet mati ≠ Bitcoin hilang", but access is the weak point: *Bitcoin bisa bertahan. Tapi bisakah lo mengaksesnya?*
- **Real assets:** GOV.UK page, NCSC-NL alert, Guardian report, Blockstream Satellite page, Bitnodes node coordinates, mempool.space block data. All sourced; nothing needed from the user.

## Retention brief (V3)
- **Audience:** Indonesian crypto holders 18–40.
- **Consequence:** If outages spread, can you still *use* the Bitcoin you own?
- **Counterintuitive fact:** Bitcoin can keep running through an outage and your coins stay on the chain, yet you may still be unable to move them; and there is a satellite that broadcasts the blockchain with no internet at all.
- **Evidence:** GOV.UK 22 Oct 2026 ("three days of disruption"; power, water, phones and the internet); Guardian 22 Oct 2026 (millions of attempts, per Cloudflare); NCSC-NL 21–23 Oct 2026 (sites, including its own, taken offline as a precaution); Blockstream Satellite (one-way broadcast).
- **One-sentence payoff:** An internet outage doesn't erase Bitcoin, but it can cut you off from using it; ownership and access are different things.
- **Misconception:** "No internet = Bitcoin gone" and "decentralised = immune to outages".
- **Share trigger:** a friend who keeps everything on an exchange app, or who thinks Bitcoin is outage-proof.
