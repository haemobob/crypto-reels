# Hook Lab — Bitcoin akan segera shutdown?

The user's brief fixes the hook ("BITCOIN AKAN SEGERA SHUTDOWN?") and the cold-open order. This lab records the alternatives considered and why the brief's hook works.

## Opening archetype
- **Archetype:** News/Document, with a realistic-render subject first (the Bitcoin coin, 0–2.3 s) because the brief requires the subject to be clear instantly.
- **Real-world anchor:** GOV.UK news story, 22 Oct 2026 (screenshot, Crown copyright text under OGL v3.0); then NCSC-NL and Guardian clippings at 9–19 s.
- **Designed form:** the page becomes a physical document in a 3D newsroom; its "three days" line becomes the monumental 72 JAM.
- **Asset hierarchy:** level 4 (realistic 3D Bitcoin coin) for 2.3 s, then level 3 (real document) from 2.4 s, so something real is on screen by second 3, within the opening rules' window.

| # | Entry point | Cold open (spoken) | First-frame headline + anchor | C | N | S | E | V | Total | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question | "BITCOIN akan segera SHUTDOWN?" | BITCOIN AKAN SEGERA SHUTDOWN? + 3D coin losing connections | 5 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 20 | Instant subject and stakes; evidence follows at 2.4 s. Must read as a question, not news. |
| 2 | Authority | "Pemerintah Inggris minta warganya siap tiga hari tanpa layanan penting." | INGGRIS: SIAP 72 JAM TANPA LAYANAN + GOV.UK page | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 21 | Most credible, but Bitcoin only arrives at 30 s. |
| 3 | Evidence | "Jutaan percobaan serangan siber menyasar PHP minggu ini." | JUTAAN SERANGAN KE PHP + Guardian clipping | 2 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 18 | Strong news; weak link to the viewer's money. |
| 4 | Simulation | "Bayangin: ATM mati, aplikasi bank nggak kebuka. Bitcoin lo?" | ATM OFFLINE. BITCOIN LO? + ATM (SIMULASI) | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 18 | Personal, but a simulation first weakens trust. |
| 5 | Contradiction | "Internet mati belum tentu bikin Bitcoin mati." | INTERNET MATI ≠ BITCOIN MATI | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 17 | Gives away the reversal. |

**Selected hook:** #1 (the user's brief), immediately substantiated by #2's evidence at 2.4 s. Together they score highest in the first 8 s.
**Why it wins:** the subject (Bitcoin) and the stake (can it stop?) are clear in one second, and the real document arrives before the viewer can doubt it.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #4. **Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #2.
**Genuine surprise check:** yes. Coins can't be erased by an outage, yet you can still be locked out; and a satellite broadcasts the blockchain.

## A/B hook variants (same body, 0–8 s)
- **A — consequence:** "Kalau internet mati, Bitcoin lo masih bisa dipakai?" over the wallet going DISCONNECTED.
- **B — authority:** the release version (question + GOV.UK 72 JAM).
- Variant used: B, per the user's brief. A is noted for a later Trial Reels test if the account supports it.
