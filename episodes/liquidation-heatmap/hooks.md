# Hook Lab — Peta likuidasi Bitcoin

## Opening archetype
- **Archetype**: Asset / Market.
- **Real-world anchor**: today's CoinGlass Binance BTC/USDT liquidation heatmap (screenshot, 21 Oct 2026 14:42 UTC).
- **Designed form it turns into**: a vertical price ladder with stacked liquidation ticks (ILUSTRASI).
- **Asset hierarchy level**: 3 (real interface screenshot).

| # | Entry point | Cold open (as spoken) | First-frame headline + anchor | C | N | S | E | V | Total | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem/authority of the tool | "Banyak trader futures cek peta ini sebelum entry: liquidation heatmap." | TRADER CEK PETA INI SEBELUM ENTRY + heatmap | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 20 | Matches the reference's promise; real map lands at 1.5 s |
| 2 | Evidence | "Hari ini ada dua garis kuning di peta likuidasi Bitcoin: delapan puluh dua ribu tiga ratus dan delapan puluh empat ribu delapan ratus." | DUA ZONA KUNING HARI INI | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 21 | Very specific, but numbers before context lose non-traders |
| 3 | Contradiction | "Garis kuning ini bukan order. Tapi harga sering ditarik ke sana." | BUKAN ORDER, TAPI MENARIK HARGA | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 19 | Strong surprise; "ditarik" too strong as an opener |
| 4 | Question | "Bitcoin mau ke delapan puluh dua ribu atau delapan puluh lima ribu dulu?" | NAIK ATAU TURUN DULU? | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 16 | The reference's own hook; invites a prediction we won't make |
| 5 | Simulation | "Bayangin kamu long lima puluh kali di delapan puluh empat ribu. Harga likuidasimu ada di zona kuning." | POSISI KAMU DI ZONA KUNING? | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 20 | Most personal; needs the map explained first |

**Selected hook:** #1, then #2's numbers as the substantiation beat.
**Why it wins:** a stranger understands it from the headline and the map; the numbers arrive by second 5.
**Broader alternative:** #3. **Literate alternative:** #5.
**Genuine surprise:** the bands are estimates of other traders' liquidation prices, not orders, and real liquidations are usually smaller.

## A/B (0–8 s)
- **A, consequence:** #5 "Bayangin kamu long lima puluh kali…"
- **B, authority/evidence (selected):** #1 + #2.

Headline alone on black at frame 0 (corner logo bug also on, per the 21 Oct decision); real heatmap by ~1.5 s; designed ladder only after ~9 s.
