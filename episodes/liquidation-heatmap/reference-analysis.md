# Reference breakdown — @pushkarrajthakur, "How Smart Traders Use Bitcoin Liquidation Heatmap"

- Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUqKNujAfCV/ (posted 12 Feb 2026; 14,363 likes, 1,321 comments at download on 21 Oct 2026). Hindi narration; 1080×1920, 60 fps, 1:54.
- Used as a **structure and pacing reference only**. Nothing from it (footage, audio, graphics, wording, numbers, branding) appears in our Reel.

## What it does
| Time | Beat | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–11 s | Hook: "BTC just had a big fall, it's at $67K. Up or down from here?" | Talking head, then a quick price-chart cutaway |
| 11–21 s | The tool: "search *liquidation heatmap*; price is more likely to move toward liquidations" | Google results for CoinGlass, then talking head |
| 21–45 s | Worked example, 12 h view: the yellow band at ~$66.8–66.9K got taken; next bands $66K / $68K | Split screen: CoinGlass heatmap recording on top, presenter below |
| 45–70 s | Longer timeframe (1 week): dark green/yellow = more liquidity; targets $72K, or $65K first then $72K | Split screen |
| 70–103 s | **Sponsored segment** for an Indian exchange (fees, leverage, a trading challenge) | Exchange marketing screens |
| 103–114 s | CTA: "comment YES for the link, share this" | Talking head, SHARE graphic |

## Why it holds attention
- A live question in the first 3 s (up or down?) answered with a concrete tool by 15 s.
- Real interface on screen (the heatmap) with a specific number every few seconds.
- A visible "it happened" moment: a band that was hit, then the next targets.

## What it misses (and our version fixes)
- Presents bands as **targets** ("Bitcoin can go to $72K"): no caveat that the map is an *estimate* (CoinGlass: actual liquidations are often lower; read it as relative).
- Never says *why* bands exist (leveraged positions' liquidation prices) or why price reacts (forced market orders).
- 40% of the runtime is an ad and a comment-bait CTA.

## Our adaptation
Same promise ("the map traders check before entering"), same real-interface anchor (today's CoinGlass heatmap), but faceless Cryptonite motion graphics, 45–55 s, mechanism explained, caveats at the claim, and a payoff the viewer can use on their own position. No talking head, no sponsor, no CTA.
