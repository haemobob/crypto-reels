## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for**: Indonesian crypto futures traders (and spot holders who see "liquidation heatmap" posts).
- **Problem / misconception**: "Zona kuning = target harga." They read an estimate as a forecast and don't know what the bands are made of.
- **What exactly they learn**: (1) a band is a cluster of *estimated* liquidation prices of leveraged positions; (2) why price is often drawn to it (forced market orders); (3) two limits: it's an estimate, and swept bands often reverse; (4) one check: keep your own liquidation price out of the bright bands.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor**: leverage trading is common; knowing where forced orders cluster protects your own position.
- **What they keep**: a concrete pre-entry check.
