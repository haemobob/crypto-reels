# Visual brief — Tokenisasi: internet 1996-nya keuangan?

- **Framework**: Remotion. The reference reel (0x100x, DUEmp0Ck0j7) was used for analysis only (download, transcript, frames); see `reference-analysis.md`.
- **Visual subject**: Larry Fink is genuinely central (the "1996" line is his, from his own 2026 letter), so the opening uses the Authority archetype with his official BlackRock headshot. After the opening the subject becomes a real token: **BUIDL, BlackRock's own tokenized fund on Ethereum**, rendered as a 3D coin with its real face (the BlackRock wordmark, white on black, as in the token's icon).
- **Visual concept**: *the same ownership, on a different ledger*, in one continuous 3D world (React Three Fiber via `@remotion/three`). A real 1880s paper share certificate ("transferable only on the Books of the Company") lies on a dark table; a scan line sweeps it away and the BUIDL coin rises out of it. The coin then races a paper share down two lanes (BEI T+2 vs blockchain), lands, and the real BUIDL transfer ledger (late-night WIB timestamps) appears. Then it splits into eight slices and one slice is pledged into a vault for a loan. On the off-white chapter, a to-scale 3D tower (BlackRock = 100%) next to a 0,25% slab (every tokenized real asset).
- **User feedback (22 Oct 2026)**: the first version's flat "TOKEN" circle and drawn certificate were too generic and too static. Replaced with a real token, a real certificate, real on-chain data, 3D and varied camera angles.
- **Different from recent episodes** (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`): no price chart at all (btc-mirip-2023, liquidation-heatmap were chart-led); an authority card with a real photo; rupiah counter with a PDB block comparison; a two-lane settlement race; a to-scale bar comparison on off-white; a numbered checklist ending.
- **Opening asset hierarchy**: level 1 (official photo, BlackRock press asset, credit on screen). The second photo the user supplied is not used: its source and licence are unknown. The user said both photos were references only.
- **Currency rule** (user decision 22 Oct 2026): BlackRock's AUM is a "how big" figure, so it is spoken and shown in rupiah (≈ Rp279.465 triliun, at 18.030 IDR/USD, 22 Oct 2026) with $15,5 triliun beside it; tokenized-asset totals are a crypto market size, so they stay in USD ($39,02 miliar). The comparison between them is a percentage (≈ 0,25%, spoken "kurang dari setengah persen").
- **Polarity**: navy/black for the promise and mechanism; off-white for the reality check (31.8–44.8 s), the turn from hype to size; back to dark for the checklist.
- **Camera angles** (cue-keyed, `src/three/camera.tsx`): high 3/4 desk shot with push-in on the certificate → eye-level on the rising coin → behind-the-lanes race view → hard cut to a reverse angle from beyond the finish line (empty floor under the ledger panel) → top-down on the split → tilt to 3/4 for the pledge → low close-up on the 0,25% slab → crane up and back to reveal the tower.
- **Hero moves**: the certificate → coin scan (18.7 s) and the tower crane (38 s). The headline clears during the crane so text never crosses the tower.
- **Framing rule used**: portrait frame is narrow (horizontal view ≈ 0.52 × distance at fov 50), so every 3D subject is framed numerically to sit in the stage (y 720–1450) with a view offset; nothing 3D enters the headline zone except fogged-out floor.
- **Left out from the reference**: the $10T figure (outdated; filing says $15.5T), "half of China's GDP" (replaced by PDB Indonesia in rupiah), US T+2 (replaced by BEI T+2), the $16T forecast (forecast, not used), the talking head.
