## What's outdated or unsupported (fixed in ours)
- "$10 trillion": BlackRock's AUM is **$15.5 trillion** at 30 Sep 2026 (SEC 8-K exhibit 99.2).
- "Two days to settle": US stocks moved to T+1 in May 2024. For our audience, the IDX is T+2 (since 26 Nov 2018).
- "$16 trillion": a 2022 BCG/ADDX *forecast for 2030*, presented as if it were money already moving. Today's distributed tokenized RWA value is **$39.02B** (rwa.xyz, 22 Oct 2026).
- No caveat at all. Fink's own 2026 letter adds one: it "won't replace the existing financial system overnight".
- Ends on a follow CTA instead of something the viewer can use.

## Our adaptation
Same grammar (authority → scale → mechanism → implication → payoff), Indonesian anchors (IDR, PDB Indonesia, BEI T+2), Fink's own primary-source words, a reality check with live data, and a 3-question check for any "tokenized real-world asset" offered to the viewer.
