# Brief — satu-meja

- **Visual subject:** the three leaders (real Kremlin.ru photos from the meeting) and the map where each shift happens. The story is people plus geography, so both carry it.
- **Visual concept:** "one table, one moving map".
  - A real satellite world map (NASA Blue Marble) in its own window travels from Shenzhen to Japan, the Atlantic, Europe, the Sea of Japan and Taiwan as each shift is named. Countries light up and pins mark the places.
  - Real photos open and close the film: the three standing together, the round table, three portraits, the three in profile. Real photos also cover Macron and US forces in Japan.
  - Each claim gets a Cryptonite report card with its outlet and date. We never imitate a publisher's masthead.
- **Different from recent episodes:**
  - The first map-led Reel.
  - The first to use real photos of people.
  - Not a chart. Navy ground with a satellite map window.
  - Several camera flights between regions; gold, blue and white mark the three powers.
- **Framework:** Remotion.
- **Hero move:** the pull-back from Shenzhen to the Pacific view at "Tapi di luar ruangan itu, peta dunia sedang bergeser".
- **Asset hierarchy:** level 1, real photos (Kremlin.ru CC BY 4.0, EU CC BY 4.0, US Marines public domain); a real satellite map (NASA, public domain).
- **Illustrative parts (labelled on screen):** the diesel route, the aircraft tracks and the ship positions.
