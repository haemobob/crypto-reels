## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian crypto traders who use candlestick patterns, above all newer ones who buy "on the hammer".
- **Viewer's problem, question or misconception:** "A hammer is a reversal signal, so I buy it." They judge the candle by its outer shape only.
- **What exactly they learn:**
  - how to open a hammer on the 5-minute chart and check two things:
    - did it **sweep** an old low (and close back above it)?
    - did it **break the last lower high** inside the hour (a change of character)?
  - that this tells you who took control, not where price goes next;
  - that the swept low is the line where the idea is wrong.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** BTC trades 24 hours. Many local traders scalp H1 setups on Indodax, Tokocrypto or Binance, and "buy the hammer" is one of the first rules they learn.
- **What they keep afterwards:** a two-question check (sweep? lower high broken?) and one risk rule: if the swept low breaks again, the idea is void.

## Discovery sequence
- **Early useful insight (8–11 s):** "di chart lima menit, isinya beda total": the three candles really are the same shape; what differs is inside.
- **Visual demonstration (11–32 s):** each hammer's real 12 five-minute candles drawn in time order, with stop orders under the old low getting swept and the lower-high gate breaking (or not).
- **Final takeaway (49–58 s):** the inside tells you who is in control, not the next direction; use the swept low as your invalidation.
- **Real assets the mechanism needs:** real BTCUSDT H1 and 5m candles (Binance public data, downloaded). Nothing needed from the user.

## Retention brief (V3)
- **Audience:** Indonesian crypto traders who trade candlestick patterns on H1.
- **Consequence:** two hammers that look identical can be built completely differently, and buying both the same way is how traders get stopped out.
- **Counterintuitive fact:**
  - the three hammers stacked on top of each other are nearly identical;
  - yet in our six-month count, the "good" pattern did not go up more often (5/8 vs 13/20).
- **Evidence:**
  - three real BTC H1 hammers: 1 Sep, 15 Oct and 17 Jul 2026;

- **One-sentence payoff:** the inside of a candle tells you who took control, not where price goes next. So use it to know where you are wrong.
