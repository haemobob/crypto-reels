## Filled 21 Nov 2026
- **Visual subject:** three pictogram traders (roles, not real people) and three real BTCUSDT H1 hammers (Binance). Each hammer's inside is the real 5m data.
- **Visual concept:** "same skin, different insides". Style per `reference/pictogram-storytelling-study.md`.
  - Black ground, white pictogram actors, brand blue as the only accent, red only for a loss.
  - The swept level is a line with stop orders (dots) under it that get taken.
  - The last lower high is a gate.
  - "Pemenang tiap 5 menit" is an ISOTYPE row: one actor per 5m candle, blue for buyers, grey for sellers.
  - The spoken sentence builds at the top (the sentence track); micro captions are off.
- **Different from recent episodes:** the first pictogram-actor episode, the first sentence-builder caption track, and the first ISOTYPE count grids (5/8 vs 13/20).
- **Seams (5 kinds):**
  - zoom through candle A into its inside ("lima menit");
  - whip A → C → B;
  - blur swap back to the traders for the results;
  - a dip and colour flood into the data twist;
  - a push into B's chart for the payoff;
  - a blur swap back to the opening shot, so the Reel loops.
- **The living frame:** every beat sits in a `Shot` drift; candles draw on in real time order; actors pop in per 5m candle; stop dots get swept; counters and grids light up on the spoken numbers.
