# Hook Lab — tiga-hammer

## Three storylines (playbook §6)
1. **Three actors, three choices (picked).**
   - Three pictogram traders each buy a hammer that looks the same. We open each candle, watch buyers and sellers inside, see each trader's result, then the data twist, the risk rule, and a loop back to the three traders.
   - Device: one object (a hammer) × three insides. Form: a zoom-through into each candle on one continuous canvas.
2. **A myth turned over.** "Hammer = reversal?" Then the 28-hammer count, then the inside view as the real use. Good, but it opens on a debate rather than a picture.
3. **Duel.** "Mana yang lebih mungkin naik: hammer A atau B?" Then evidence. Weaker: the honest answer is "barely different", which kills a duel hook.

## Opening archetype
- **Asset/Market.** The real anchor is three real BTC H1 hammers (Binance, with dates) shown side by side as candles, each beside its trader.

- **Selected:** #1. A picture that makes the claim, a real anchor at frame 0, and the loop ("isinya beda total") opens at about 4 s.
- **Re-hooks:**
  - about 5 s: "isinya beda total";
  - about 30 s: "Tapi ini bukan rumus" (the twist, paid at once by the 5/8 vs 13/20 count).
- **A/B variants:** A (problem, #3) vs B (contradiction, #1, used).
