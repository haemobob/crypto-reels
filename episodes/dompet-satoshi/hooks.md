# Hook Lab — dompet-satoshi

## Opening archetype
- **Archetype:** News/Document (the Binance hack), turning into Asset/Market (Satoshi's coins on-chain).
- **Real-world anchor:**
  - the real headline of TRM Labs' report ("North Korea Steals USD 6.36 Billion From Binance in the Largest Hack in History", 30 Oct 2026), shown in a Cryptonite panel with its source;
  - the official Binance logo.
- **Designed form it turns into:** a theft counter, then a comparison bar of 18,270 BTC vs ~1,097,650 BTC.
- **Asset hierarchy level:** 2–3 (logo, document headline, real on-chain numbers). There is no photo of Satoshi, and none will be invented.

Scoring 0–5: C consequence · N novelty · S specificity · E evidence · V visual.

**Selected hook:** #1, built with #6's loop.
**Why:** a concrete, verified number and time; something real on screen by about 1.5 s; the contradiction by about 5 s; the first answer by about 10 s.
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #4 (consequence first).
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #2 (the contradiction).
**Genuine surprise:** yes. About 60 times the BTC that was stolen sits visibly on-chain, untouched for almost 18 years.

## A/B variants (0–8 s)
- **A (consequence):** "Kalau kripto lo di exchange, kuncinya bukan di tangan lo. Akhir Oktober, Binance kehilangan enam koma tiga miliar dolar…"
- **B (scale / contradiction, selected):** "Enam koma tiga miliar dolar. Sebagian besar dikuras dari Binance cuma dalam dua puluh menit…"
- Silent comparison: first-frame stills of A and B at preview stage.
- Used: B. It saves the "exchange holds your keys" line for the payoff, so the ending answers the opening.

## First-frame test
Headline "$6,3 MILIAR DICURI" with "±20 MENIT" under it, rising in. Narration starts on frame 0–3. The Binance logo and the TRM headline arrive by about 1.5 s. The 3D field of locks waits until well after 8 s.
