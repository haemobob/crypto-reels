# Brief — emas-bank-sentral

- **Framework:** Remotion + React Three Fiber (one ThreeCanvas, long-lens isometric camera, fov 7–23°, near/far 60–240 for depth precision, dpr 2 supersampling).
- **Format source:** the user asked to recreate the Argus Terminal reel (20 Nov 2026) "type and design". Adapted, not copied: our own models, layout, type (Inter Tight), colours (Cryptonite blue world, gold only for gold), our logo on a navy plate, our script and corrected data. No reference frames, audio or assets are used; the reference video stays in the scratchpad, not the repo.
- **Visual subject:** gold itself (bars, crates, a pile) and the buyers and sellers as places in a city: the speculators' tower, the US bond building, the central bank vault, China's convoy, four central banks with signs.
- **Visual concept:** *one city where money is physical.* Each beat is a place on the same main road; trucks never stop driving; numbers are written on the ground.
- **Real anchor:** the real gold price (daily closes) on a billboard from frame 0, and the real October drop on a second billboard.
- **Different from recent episodes:** first isometric miniature-city world; first gold/macro story since jpmorgan-bitcoin; bright monochrome blue ground instead of dark studios; a karaoke caption box (bottom) instead of the top sentence track, to match the format the user asked for.
- **Captions:** bottom karaoke box (y ≈ 1430–1560, inside the safe zone), current word in gold. `captions: "none"` in episode.json (the box is drawn by the episode).
- **Logo:** official logo on a navy plate (`logoPlate`) so the blue gem reads on the blue ground.
