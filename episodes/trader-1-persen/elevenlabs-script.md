# ElevenLabs script — 1% yang nggak nyerah?

**Model:** Eleven v3. **Voice:** Ongky Himawan – Calm, Deep. **Stability:** Natural.

Paste the whole block into one generation and send the MP3. Words in [brackets] are v3 delivery tags (not spoken). "..." = a held beat. No CAPS. It must read word for word like this, so the captions line up.

```
[intrigued, slowly] Katanya... kalau nggak pernah nyerah, lo bakal jadi satu persen trader yang menang.
[curious] Ada yang ngecek. Hampir dua puluh ribu orang mulai day trading futures di Brasil.
[explaining] Yang cuma sehari: hampir tiga puluh persen untung. Yang bertahan seratus sampai dua ratus hari: hampir tujuh persen. [serious] Lebih dari tiga ratus hari... cuma tiga persen.
[knowingly] Makin lama bertahan, makin sedikit yang untung. Dan nggak ada bukti mereka makin jago.
[calm, confident] Jadi sebelum bilang jangan nyerah, cek dulu: setelah biaya, hasil lo udah positif belum?
```

## If a word sounds wrong
| Word | Say it like |
|---|---|
| trader | tre-der |
| day trading | dei tre-ding |
| futures | fyu-cers |

Target length: about 30–36 s (the Reel allows 25–40 s). If the voice reads the first tag aloud, keep the take: the extra word is cut in the edit.
