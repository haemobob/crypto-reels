# Brief — 1% yang nggak nyerah?

- **Topic:** the viral "be among the 1% who refuse to give up" clip (GoddyFX, 11 Nov 2026), answered with the one dataset that follows every beginner day trader in a market (Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti; 19,646 people, Brazil, 2013–2015).
- **Viewer payoff:** persistence isn't an edge: the longer people kept day trading, the smaller the share in profit; check net results after costs first.
- **Runtime:** 30.9 s narration (Orus working voice) + 0.6 s hold + 2 s outro; window 25–40 s (close to the reference's short loop).
- **Framework:** Remotion, 2D SVG.

## Visual subject
Day traders as three stick figures shooting at three hoops: the reference's picture, kept because it is the clearest metaphor for "trying again and again". Each figure is labelled with a real group from the study. Then the study itself (a designed card with its real title, authors, institution, data source) and its real six-group bar chart.

## Visual concept: the court
A navy court under a blue spotlight, three lanes stacked. Shots that go in are an illustration of each group's percentage (labelled); the percentages are data. The court gives way to the real chart, and ends on the only scoreboard that matters: net result after costs, positive or negative.

## Different from recent episodes (`reference/episode-visual-log.md`)
- First figurative episode: characters (stick figures) instead of a chart, board or 3D world; a document card as the real anchor (News/Document archetype).
- Short format (≈ 33 s) against 64–95 s for the last four.
- **Hero move:** none needed; the lanes light up one by one and the bar chart grows (one continuous build).
