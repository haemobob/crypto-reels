## Viewer reward
- **Who it is for:** Indonesian retail traders (crypto and others) who hear "don't give up, be the 1%" from trading content and courses.
- **Viewer's problem or misconception:** that persistence alone turns day traders into winners.
- **What exactly they learn:** in the one dataset that follows every beginner (19,646 people, Brazil, 2013–2015), the share in net profit fell the longer people kept day trading: 29.8% after 1 day, 6.8% at 101–200 days, 3.0% after more than 300 days; and the authors found no evidence they got better.
- **Why it matters to an Indonesian crypto investor:** "jangan nyerah" is used to keep people paying for courses and signals; checking net results after costs is the honest test.

## Retention brief (V3)
- **Audience:** Indonesian retail traders, 18–35, crypto and futures.
- **Consequence:** "never give up" can mean "keep losing longer".
- **Counterintuitive fact:** those who persisted were *less* likely to be in profit (29.8% → 3.0%).
- **Evidence:** Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti (FEA-USP, 2019/2020), CVM data on all 19,646 beginners in mini-Ibovespa futures, 2013–2015 (S1–S3).
- **One-sentence payoff:** persistence isn't an edge; check whether your results are positive after costs.
- **Misconception:** "the 1% are the ones who didn't quit".
