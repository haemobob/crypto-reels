# Hook Lab — trader-1-persen

## Opening archetype (opening rules)
- **Archetype:** News/Document (a study) with the claim as a visual: three traders shooting hoops.
- **Real-world anchor:** the study card (real title, authors, institution, data source, sample size) at 4.7 s.
- **Designed form:** the card hands its numbers to three labelled lanes, then to a real six-bar chart.
- **Asset hierarchy level used:** 3 (document / real data).

| # | Entry point | Cold open (as spoken) | First-frame headline | C | N | S | E | V | Total | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question (the claim) | "Katanya, kalau nggak pernah nyerah, lo bakal jadi satu persen trader yang menang." | 1% YANG NGGAK NYERAH? | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 18 | Mirrors the reference's line the viewer already knows |
| 2 | Contradiction | "Makin lama orang day trading, makin sedikit yang untung." | MAKIN LAMA, MAKIN RUGI? | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 21 | The twist; works best after the claim |
| 3 | Evidence | "Dari dua puluh ribu day trader, yang bertahan lebih dari tiga ratus hari: cuma tiga persen untung." | 3% | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 21 | Strong number, weaker picture |
| 4 | Problem | "Udah setahun trading, masih rugi? Ini datanya." | MASIH RUGI? | 5 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 15 | Personal but generic |
| 5 | Authority | "Peneliti Brasil ngecek semua day trader pemula." | 19.646 TRADER | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 19 | Credible, less emotional |

**Selected hook:** #1 (the claim itself, framed "katanya"), with #5 as the substantiation (4.7 s) and #2 as the pay-off line (20.4 s).
**Alternative for broader audiences:** #4.
**Alternative for financially literate viewers:** #3.
**Genuine surprise check:** yes: persistence correlated with *fewer* profitable traders.

## A/B hook variants (same body, 0–8 s)
- **Variant A — consequence:** #4.
- **Variant B — claim/contradiction (released):** #1.
