# Cryptonite opening rules: 0x100x-style openings (17 Oct 2026)

Applies to the first 3–12 seconds of every Reel. Works inside the retention rules (`cryptonite-retention.md`, Hook Lab and 0–12 s cold open) and the visual rules (`cryptonite-reels.md`). Where those files are less specific about the opening, this file decides.

## Goal
The opening reads as **premium financial editorial + real-world evidence + cinematic design**, not as a generic animated explainer. The viewer should first feel "this is real", "this matters", "this is important", and only then "this is well designed". In the opening, credibility and immediacy come before graphics.

## Opening order (every Reel)
1. **Statement.** A strong headline, scale statement or contradiction in large, clean typography (centred, rising in from below, 3–8 words).
2. **Real-world anchor.** Immediately anchor the topic in something recognisably real: an actual person photo, company logo, product or asset image, chart screenshot, document or article clipping, or map / currency / commodity / market visual.
3. **Integration.** Transform or integrate that real asset into the designed motion-graphics system: a profile card, company card, institutional panel, visual diagram or chart environment.
4. **Expansion.** Only after that does the film move into abstract explanatory graphics (3D, diagrams, flows).

The first 3–8 seconds are anchored in something recognisably real. Never open on pure abstract 3D shapes or generic motion graphics.

## The three questions
By the end of the cold open the viewer can answer:
1. Who or what is this about?
2. Why is it important?
3. Why should I keep watching?

## Archetypes (pick one per Reel and record it in `hooks.md`)
The examples below illustrate each technique; they are not subjects to reuse. Pick the archetype the story calls for (`cryptonite-story-visuals.md`).
**1. Authority Hook**: when the topic is driven by a person, institution or major company.
Bold statement → real photo or logo → profile card or institutional card → scale metric → explanation.
Example: "HE MANAGES $10 TRILLION" → Larry Fink photo → BlackRock-style profile card → scale number counts up → why it matters.

**2. Asset / Market Hook**: when the topic is driven by a coin, a chart move or a market event.
Bold contradiction or problem statement → real chart, coin or exchange screenshot or relevant visual → transition into a clean designed chart or system diagram → explanation.
Example: "BITCOIN TURUN 5%. ALTCOIN LO ANJLOK 20%." → realistic chart or market-drop visual → stylised market-structure diagram → liquidity, leverage, beta.

**3. News / Document Hook**: when the topic is driven by an announcement, proposal or breaking news.
Bold headline → real headline clipping, official document or news-style panel → structured explainer visuals → explanation.
Example: "INDONESIA BISA KELUAR DARI INDEKS GLOBAL" → official MSCI announcement card, or a Reuters/CNBC-style news card → classification diagram → consequences.

## Visual asset hierarchy
When available, use visuals in this order:
1. real photos
2. real logos
3. real screenshots, charts, documents
4. realistic rendered objects
5. abstract geometric graphics

Use the highest level that exists for the topic. Drop a level only when nothing real is available, and say why in `brief.md`.

## Motion in the opening
Restrained: small push-ins, card reveals, subtle light sweeps, elegant reframing, clean typography timing. Do not over-animate. The credibility of the opening matters more than spectacle; save hero moves for the body.

## Real assets: sourcing and honesty
- Every real asset (photo, logo, screenshot, clipping) is listed in `sources.md` with where it came from, its date, and its licence or the basis for using it (official press kit, the company's own press release, editorial use of a public document).
- Prefer official sources: press kits, the institution's own site and releases, exchange or index-provider pages. Show documents whole and readable first, then zoom into the narrated part (layout rules).
- Third-party logos and photos identify the subject; they are never altered to imply endorsement, partnership or a quote the person didn't give. "Style" cards (e.g. "Reuters/CNBC-style") are Cryptonite-designed panels that carry the real headline and its source; they never imitate a publisher's masthead or pass as the publisher's own graphic.
- If a real asset can't be obtained or cleared, use the next level of the hierarchy and say so to the user, rather than inventing a likeness.
- Chart screenshots that are too low-resolution may be rebuilt as vectors with identical values (noted in `sources.md`).
